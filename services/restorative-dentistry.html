restorative dentistry in South Tampa, Fl

Cosmetic Veneers

Our restorative dentistry in Tampa includes custom-shaded veneers. Cosmetic veneers are thin restorations that are like a facade that are bonded to the front of your teeth, providing immediate coverage to address a variety of dental imperfections. Whether you have chipped, stained, misaligned, or unevenly spaced teeth, veneers can create a stunning, symmetrical, and natural-looking smile.

Composites (Tooth Colored Fillings)

Gone are the days of noticeable metal dental fillings in South Tampa. At Upshaw Dental Studio, we prioritize aesthetics and offer tooth- colored fillings (composites) through our expert restorative dentistry in Tampa, FL. Made from high quality ceramic materials, these restorations blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a natural and beautiful appearance. Whether you need to treat a small cavity or to address a bigger problem, our tooth-colored composites ensure that your dental work is virtually undetectable.

Porcelain Onlays/inlays

Porcelain onlays have emerged as a stronger, durable and more aesthetically pleasing solution for restoring damaged or decayed teeth. Porcelain onlays, also known as partial crowns, are a type of dental restoration that lies between a dental filling and a full crown. In terms of coverage they offer patients an effective alternative to traditional dental treatments, combining the benefits of durability, functionality, and natural aesthetics.

Porcelain onlays are designed to restore and repair teeth that have sustained moderate damage or decay, particularly when the affected area is too large for a regular filling but not extensive enough to warrant a full crown, therefor preserving more of the natural structure of the tooth.

Crafted from high-quality dental porcelain, this type of restorative dental care in Tampa offerings custom-made solutions to match the natural color and shape of the patient's teeth. The porcelain material closely resembles the natural tooth enamel, providing both functional and aesthetic benefits.

Porcelain Crowns

Porcelain crowns play a crucial role in restorative dentistry by protecting and strengthening damaged or weakened teeth. A crown is a custom-made cover that is placed over a tooth to restore its shape, size, and strength, but looks like a natural tooth whenever you’re smiling. Whether you have a severely compromised tooth or one that has recently undergone root canal therapy, this dental restoration in Tampa can provide long-lasting protection and restore your ability to eat and smile normally. Our porcelain crowns are meticulously crafted to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring a beautiful and functional result.

Fixed Dental Bridges

If you have one or two missing teeth, a fixed dental bridge in South Tampa can be an excellent restorative option. A bridge consists of a pair or more of crowns that are anchored to adjacent natural teeth or dental implants, suspending a false (pontic) crown between them. By filling the gap left by missing teeth, a dental bridge restores your ability to speak and eat properly while improving the aesthetics of your smile. At Upshaw Dental Studio, our restorative dentist in Tampa takes pride in creating custom-made dental bridges that are both durable and natural-looking, ensuring a seamless integration with your existing teeth.

Dental Implants

For a more permanent, non-invasive, and natural-looking tooth replacement, dental implants are the gold standard in restorative dentistry. Dental implants are biocompatible structures that are surgically placed to act as artificial tooth roots. Once the implants integrate with the bone, they provide a stable foundation for dental crowns or bridges on top of them. Our restorative dentist in South Tampa will walk you through every step of the treatment process, so you'll always know what to expect.
